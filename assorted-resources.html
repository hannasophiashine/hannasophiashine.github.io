Assorted Resources

A running collection of links and resources I've found useful — for grad school applications, research methods, writing, or just general interest. I've attempted to organize this to some extent but apologies if some of it is nonsensical! Feel free to reach out if you have questions about any of these!

Grad School & Applications

  • The Portable Mentor — a guide/handbook for applying to graduate school + early grad student/faculty advice. Honestly, this feels very clinical focused but some of the more general advice is helpful! Time management skills!! Important! & this is free :)
  • PPREP — a mentorship program run by Harvard faculty & students (I think?), open to all! While I did not directly participate in this program, my understanding is that you are paired with a faculty mentor or senior researcher (postdoc) and they guide you through the application process and review your application materials for you if needed. Would highly recommend, Harvard faculty are wonderful folks!
